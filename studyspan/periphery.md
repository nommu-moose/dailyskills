# Periphery

Start from their core. Read [note judgment](../dailyskills-shared/note-judgment.md). Load [lenses](../dailyskills-shared/lenses.md) only when this widening has a lens.

Teach what the core assumes, what sits next to it, and what a wider reading changes. Write the wider map under a length they set. If they have not set a length, ask before writing. Then say what to add in their own words, what to read, and what not to memorize.

Do not restate the core. Do not condense a book they have not opened. If there is no core yet, stop and hand that to `study`.
