Real summaries, or a real core, are in front of them. A heading list is not a summary. A vague list of what should be known is Skeleton in `study`, not this skill.

Read the [record rules](../dailyskills-shared/record.md). Load only the selected mode. Links are routes, not a list to read in advance.

- **Gap.** What those summaries leave uncovered. Read [gap](gap.md).
- **Periphery.** What sits outside a real core. Read [periphery](periphery.md).

If it is not obvious which, ask which they want. Do not ask them to produce notes they do not have.

Do not condense a book. Do not critique their prose. Do not write a substitute chapter. Add a pace line only when the record shows they are materially off.
