Read the [record rules](../dailyskills-shared/record.md) and resume the folder they name. An empty record is not a reason to wait. Do not search the disk for a record.

If they already asked for a condensation, a note critique, a coverage map, or a mark, stop and name `studyread`, `studynotes`, `studyspan`, or `studymark`. Do not do that job here.

If the mode is not obvious, ask only what you cannot see: what is in front of them, and what this sitting should change. Ask the lens and the length only when the next skill will write a condensation or periphery notes. Then stop asking. "Nothing, or only a rough list" is Skeleton, not `studyspan`, and not an instruction to go take notes first.

Load only the selected mode. Links are routes, not a list to read in advance.

- **Session.** They can name the concept or the stuck point. Read [session](session.md).
- **Skeleton.** Nothing is in front of them, or only a vague list of what should be known. Read [skeleton](skeleton.md).

Load [lenses](../dailyskills-shared/lenses.md) only when this sitting teaches from a source in front of them, or when handing to `studyread` or periphery without a lens yet. One primary, optional short secondary. A custom lens in one sentence is allowed.

Do not write their notes, except the narrow case the selected mode allows. Do not write a condensation, a coverage pack, or a replacement essay.
