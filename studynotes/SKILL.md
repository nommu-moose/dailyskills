name: studynotes
description: "Critique the user's own notes when they are bloated, core-less, or brute-force. They keep the pen. Do not use when they have no notes, or the notes are fine and they want the surroundings."
---

Their notes are the material. Read the [record rules](../dailyskills-shared/record.md) and [note judgment](../dailyskills-shared/note-judgment.md).

Mark what is over-explained, not the core, or verbose without analysis. Teach a way of thinking that is not their brute-force habit, from these notes only. Record a pitfall they repeat.

You may write one short contrast. Do not write a replacement set. They still write the notes.

If they do not understand the concept, say so and hand that to `study`. If they have no notes, stop. If the notes are fine and they want the surroundings, hand that to `studyspan`. Do not condense a book. Do not mark an essay.

Add a pace line only when the record shows they are materially off.
