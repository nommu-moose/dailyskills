# Lenses

Not a skill. Load this only when the active skill tells you to.

One primary. An optional secondary stays short and is labelled secondary. A custom lens in one sentence is allowed. It is the primary. Do not silently add one of the four beside it.

If they name no lens, ask before writing. Do not choose one and continue.

- **Learn.** What has to be usable later: claims, distinctions, conditions, exceptions, application.
- **Analyse.** The text as an argument: assumptions, what does the work, where it strains.
- **Situate.** Author, motive, audience, and what it is answering.
- **Close-read.** Form, diction, ambiguity, what is not said.

Less obvious notes are required when the lens earns them. Under Learn, keep those short. Do not drop what has to be usable when Learn is primary. Do not flatten an argument, a situation, or a poem when another lens is primary.

The length is a budget. Stop at it. If they name no length, ask before writing. Do not pick a length and continue.
