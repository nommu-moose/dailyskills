description: "Condense a source the user has, under one lens and a length budget. Use when that source is there and they want notes. Do not use when there is no source, or they want to be taught rather than handed a condensation."
---

A source is in front of them and they want notes from it. Read the [record rules](../dailyskills-shared/record.md), the [lenses](../dailyskills-shared/lenses.md), and [note judgment](../dailyskills-shared/note-judgment.md).

If they name no lens or no length, ask before writing. Do not pick a lens or a length for them. One primary, optional short secondary. A custom lens in one sentence is allowed. The length is a budget, not a target to fill.

Write the condensation under that lens and that budget. Less obvious notes are required when the lens earns them, and kept short under Learn.

Do not teach the sitting in place of the condensation. If they want to be taught, hand that to `study`. Do not invent a source. Do not critique their old notes. Do not mark an essay.

Add a pace line only when the record shows they are materially off.
