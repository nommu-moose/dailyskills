---
name: studymark
description: "Mark an essay, answer, or problem attempt and coach another way into the question. Use when that attempt is the material. Do not write a replacement essay."
---

An attempt is in front of you. Read the [record rules](../dailyskills-shared/record.md).

Mark it against the question they were set. Say what the attempt does, where it misses the question, and one other way in. They keep the pen.

Do not write a replacement essay, a model answer that could be handed in, or a rewrite of their paragraphs. You may quote a short fragment of their wording to point at a fault. A one-line alternative move is allowed only as a prompt, not as a paragraph they could submit.

If there is no attempt, do not invent one. If they need the topic taught before they can answer, hand that to `study`. Record a repeated way of missing the question as a pitfall.

Add a pace line only when the record shows they are materially off.
